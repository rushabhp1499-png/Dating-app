# Hinge Prompts: Rushabh

## Final 3 (use these)

**1. Unpopular opinion**
> 12 AM birthday wishes are overrated. Nobody is at their best at midnight, not even the cake. Wish me at 10 AM with a good coffee like a civilised person.

Why it works: a light opinion she can disagree with, which starts a conversation (Voss: people love to correct you).

**2. A fact about me that surprises people**
> I run a family business that sells specialty paper. Yes, like The Office. No, I'm not Michael Scott. Debatable whether I'm Jim or Dwight.

Why it works: everyone knows The Office, it says "stable businessman" without bragging, and "Jim or Dwight?" is a ready-made reply for her.

**3. Typical Sunday**
> Beach walk, fresh air, good coffee, light food. Then a game of bowling that I take far too seriously. Loser picks dinner.

Why it works: she can picture herself in it, and "loser picks dinner" is a soft invitation (Phil Jones: make the next step easy).

## Alternates (swap in to test after ~1 week)

**The way to win me over is**
> Recommend me a book I haven't read, beat me at bowling (good luck), or teach me horse riding. It's been on my list forever.

**We'll get along if**
> You don't mind that my friends call me "always busy". When I make time for someone, I mean it.

**I'm looking for**
> Someone to be silly with on a Tuesday and build a life with eventually. Bonus if you'll play football with me or at least pretend to enjoy watching it.

**My simple pleasures**
> Football on a weekday evening, a book that wrecks my brain a little, and sea breeze on a Sunday.

## Voice prompt

Use **"Give me travel tips for..."** or **"My most irrational fear..."**, recorded in your college-fest anchor voice. Something like:
> "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the most anticipated event of the year: you, swiping right on me. Please hold your applause." *(laugh)*

Keep it under 20 seconds and smile while you record. You can hear the smile.
