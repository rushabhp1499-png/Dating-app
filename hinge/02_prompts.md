# Hinge Prompts: Rushabh

## Final 3 (use these)

**1. A shower thought I recently had**
> Midnight birthday wishes are the only time it's socially acceptable to wake someone up just to remind them they're a year older.

Why it works: a light opinion she can disagree with, which starts a conversation (Voss: people love to correct you).

**2. I'm convinced that**
> Garba is cardio. I've run the full Tata Mumbai Marathon, and honestly, nine nights of Navratri is the tougher race.

Why it works: it shows culture (Garba), discipline (a full marathon) and humour in one line, which suits the community-minded girls you want. It also hands her an easy question: "Are you a Garba person?"

**3. The way to win me over is**
> Recommend a book I haven't read, out-dance me at Garba (good luck), or teach me horse riding. It's been on my list forever.

Why it works: it gives her three easy ways to start a chat, and the "(good luck)" adds playful challenge.

## Voice prompt

Use **"Give me travel tips for..."** or **"My most irrational fear..."**, recorded in your college-fest anchor voice. Something like:
> "Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the most anticipated event of the year: you, swiping right on me. Please hold your applause." *(laugh)*

Keep it under 20 seconds and smile while you record. You can hear the smile.

## Profile fields

- **Job:** Director, family business (specialty paper). Add the company name only if you're comfortable with that.
- **Dating intentions:** "Life partner" or "Long-term, open to short". The second is more honest for "marriage material but also fun" and gets more matches.
- **Height:** 6'0". Make sure it's filled in. It's a real filter on Hinge.
- **Hometown:** Mumbai. **Location:** Dahisar.
- **Languages:** Gujarati, Hindi, English, Marathi
- **Religion:** fill it in honestly. Since it matters to you, set the matching filter on your preferences.
- **Distance filter:** around 20–25 km from Dahisar covers Borivali to Andheri and Mira-Bhayandar. Widen it if matches stay low.
- **Interests:** Football, Running, Reading, Philosophy, Dancing, Beaches, Bowling

## Books: don't list these on the profile
*Never Split the Difference* is a negotiation book. On a dating profile it can read as "he'll negotiate me," and *Men Are from Mars* can feel dated to some women. Keep them for conversation: if she likes psychology, mention them warmly there.
