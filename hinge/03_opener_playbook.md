# Rushabh's Hinge Playbook

Use this to send likes and reply on your own in seconds. Come back to Claude only for the tricky ones.

## The 10-second rule for every like
1. Pick **one** specific thing from her profile (a photo or a prompt). Never comment on "you're pretty".
2. Use the matching template below and swap in her detail.
3. End with something easy to answer: a playful either/or, a guess, or a light challenge.

---

## Openers by what's on her profile

**Travel photo (mountains, beach, abroad)**
- "Okay, this view is unfair. Rate it honestly: better than Kasol in the snow, or are we pretending?"
- "I'm guessing this was a 'planned for months' trip, not a 'booked on a whim' trip. Am I right?"

**Food / café / cooking**
- "Important question before anything else: what's the one dish here you'd defend in an argument?"
- "This looks like a 'we'll split it' plate that nobody actually splits."

**Garba / traditional outfit / Navratri**
- "Chaniya choli game strong. Real question though: are you a 9-nights-straight Garba person or a 'Saturday only' one?"
- "I've run a full marathon and I still think Navratri is the tougher event. Back me up here."

**Dog / pet**
- "Your dog looks like it has strong opinions. What's its most dramatic habit?"

**Fitness / running / gym**
- "Fellow runner? I did the Tata Mumbai Marathon and my knees still send me complaints. What's your distance?"

**Books / reading**
- "What's the last book that kept you up way past bedtime? I need a recommendation that isn't on every Instagram list."

**Football / sports**
- "Please tell me you're not a Chelsea fan. This could end before it starts."

**A funny prompt / hot take**
- Agree and push it further, or disagree playfully: "Strong take. I'll respect it, but I'm going to need your reasoning in under 10 words."

**Her prompt mentions her ideal Sunday / date**
- "[Her thing] sounds perfect. One condition: I get to pick the coffee place, you get to pick the [her thing]."

**Profile with very little info (only photos)**
- Comment on the most specific thing in the most "her" photo: the place, the outfit, the activity, the expression. "That laugh in the third photo, what was so funny?"

## Once she replies

| Message # | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Match her energy. Ask about **her** (how/what questions, not yes/no). Share one small thing about you in return. |
| 4–6 | Find a shared thing (Mumbai area, Gujarati, food, travel, Garba) and make it a running joke. |
| 7–10 | **Ask her out.** Don't wait for the "perfect moment". |

**The date ask (Phil Jones style, low pressure):**
> "I'm enjoying this, but texting doesn't do it justice. Would you be against continuing this over coffee this weekend? I'm in Dahisar, happy to meet somewhere in between."

If she's slow or vague:
> "No pressure at all. Seems like you've got a packed week. What day usually works better for you?"

## Rules
- Reply when you see it, but don't send double messages. If she goes quiet for 3+ days, send one light callback to an earlier joke, then let it go.
- Keep messages short. Hers 2 lines? Yours 2 lines.
- No "hey", no "hi beautiful", no copy-paste of the same opener to everyone. Every opener has her specific detail.
- When it's tricky (she gives a one-word reply, an awkward moment, the date ask goes sideways), screenshot just that chat and send it here.
